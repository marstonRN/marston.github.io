About me
======
Before moving into academia, I spent many years at the bedside in the paediatric intermediate and intensive care units of Lausanne University Hospital (CHUV) and the University Children's Hospital Basel (UKBB), later also working as a scientific collaborator. Throughout this time, one question stayed with me: what families go through when their child is critically ill, and how we, as healthcare professionals, can best support them. This concern shaped my academic path, from a Certificate of Advanced Studies (CAS) in Child and family health in the clinical setting to a Master's degree and then a PhD in Nursing Science, and it remains at the heart of my work today. My research explores how families can be integrated into care, so that supporting the family becomes a recognised and structured part of caring for the child. An important part of my work is related to how these changes can be implemented, and more specifically, how to educate and train healthcare professional to adopt a different perspective.
