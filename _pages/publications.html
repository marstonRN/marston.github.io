Improving everyday clinical practice and quality of care requires change at every level: with patients, families and healthcare professionals at the bedside, within teams and organisations, and across the wider healthcare system. This is best achieved through two complementary pillars: well-structured, purposeful education that prepares professionals to adopt new perspectives, and solid, meaningful research necessary to evidence based practice. My publications reflect this commitment, from my doctoral work on family-centred care in paediatric intensive care to national and international contributions.
