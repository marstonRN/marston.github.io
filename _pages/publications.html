My core concern are every day clinical practice and quality of care. Improving them requires change at every level: with patients, families and healthcare professionals at the bedside (micro), within teams and organisations (meso), and across the wider health system (macro). Such change is best achieved through two complementary pillars: well-structured, purposeful [education](/teaching/) that prepares professionals to adopt new perspectives, and solid, meaningful research that provides the evidence on which practice can build. The publications below reflect this commitment, from my doctoral work on family-centred care in paediatric intensive care to my contributions at the national level.
