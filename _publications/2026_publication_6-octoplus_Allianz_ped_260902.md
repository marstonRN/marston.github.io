excerpt: 'Families of chronically critically ill (CCI) children in paediatric critical care face lasting psychological distress. OCTO-Plus, a co-developed intervention combining repeated family consultations and interprofessional handovers from day 8 until discharge, was tested in a single-centre pilot feasibility study. Of 55 eligible families, 19 (34.5%) were recruited. Families received a mean of 2.5 consultations; 73% of planned consultations were delivered and 47% of families received the full intervention. Parents reported clinically meaningful reductions in stress, anxiety and depression, and none met the PTSD threshold at follow-up. OCTO-Plus appears feasible in clinical practice and warrants an effectiveness trial.'
