excerpt: 'This study describes the development and implementation of the training program preparing interventionists to deliver OCTO-Plus in paediatric critical care. Co-developed with partner parents and clinicians, the program combined four modalities (written documentation, asynchronous e-learning, interactive role-play sessions and one-to-one coaching at the first consultation) around four content capsules. All 11 interventionists completed the training before their first consultation, described it as comprehensive, and became markedly faster with experience. The program was feasible, acceptable and adapted to its context, and a tiered approach, from general family-centred care literacy for all staff to expert coaching, appears to be the condition for reliable delivery at scale.'
