excerpt: 'This mixed-methods study tested the feasibility of OCTO-Plus, a multi-component family support intervention combining structured family consultations, interprofessional handovers and a family diary, from the perspective of healthcare professionals in paediatric critical care. Based on 58 surveys and two focus groups with 11 professionals, the intervention proved acceptable and relevant, with 73% of planned consultations delivered and consultation and handover times decreasing markedly with experience. Acceptability was limited by clinical workload, scheduling constraints and documentation volume, and family participation dropped after the first consultation. OCTO-Plus can be embedded in routine practice when leadership endorses it and absorbs the time it takes, and when the reference role is shared across the interprofessional team.'
