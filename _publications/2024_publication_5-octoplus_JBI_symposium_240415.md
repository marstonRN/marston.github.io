excerpt: "Families of chronically critically ill (CCI) children in paediatric critical care experience high stress, unmet needs and impaired family functioning. We co-developed OCTO-Plus through two focus groups with 27 healthcare professionals and three with 12 parents of former CCI patients. Both groups rated the intervention as feasible (7.6/10 and 7.9/10). Barriers included lack of time and limited flexibility; perceived need was a key facilitator. Stakeholders' input led to tailoring to the local setting and simplifying the intervention into three components: family consultations, patient and family diaries, and interprofessional handovers. Co-construction improved acceptability and feasibility, and OCTO-Plus was widely supported by all stakeholders."
