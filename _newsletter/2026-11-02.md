## Nursing and healthcare in Switzerland

On 30 October, the Swiss Nurses Association published an interactive calculator using federal estimates to compare costs for measures in package 2 of the nursing initiative: estimates range from CHF 670 million to CHF 2.05 billion, while a 10% reduction in turnover could save CHF 200–300 million ([read the calculator](https://sbk-asi.ch/de/aktuell/was-bessere-arbeitsbedingungen-in-der-pflege-kosten-und-was-sie-sparen), 30 October).
