## Swiss health policy

Swissinfo reports that pharmaceutical companies and industry groups are pressing for higher medicine prices in Europe, including Switzerland, arguing that price pressure may affect product launches and innovation. The analysis describes how US Most-Favoured Nation pricing rules have changed, while the effect on Switzerland remains uncertain. ## Nursing and healthcare worldwide

- The ICN's Alliance of Student and Early Career Nurses has been appointed to the WHO Youth Council for a two-year term. The council's initial priorities include equity for young people in nursing, health education and literacy, and planetary health. [Nursing Times](https://www.nursingtimes.net/newly-registered-nurses/who-to-be-advised-by-early-career-nursing-group-15-10-2026/) (15 October).
- At England's Lampard inquiry into mental-health deaths, lawyers for bereaved families urged a national halt to Oxevision monitoring cameras on inpatient wards, citing patient safety, privacy, and the risk of remote monitoring substituting for in-person care. The company disputed claims that the system was ineffective or unsafe. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/society/2026/oct/20/ban-intrusive-cameras-mental-health-wards-england-bereaved-families) (20 October).
