## Swiss health policy

Swissinfo reports that pharmaceutical companies and industry groups are pressing for higher medicine prices in Europe, including Switzerland, after United States Most-Favoured Nation pricing reforms. The analysis says implementation of the United States framework appears narrower than initially expected, while wider effects remain uncertain ([read the analysis](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/drug-pricing/pharma-pushes-for-higher-european-drug-prices-trump-or-no-trump/92242926), 21 October). The Federal Council and FOPH listings yielded no additional relevant health-policy announcement dated 15–22 October ([Federal Council](https://www.admin.ch/en/newnsb); [FOPH](https://www.bag.admin.ch/en/current)).

## Nursing and healthcare worldwide

The ICN Alliance of Student and Early Career Nurses has been appointed to the WHO Youth Council for a two-year term. Initial priorities include equity for young people in nursing, health education and literacy, and planetary health ([read the report](https://www.nursingtimes.net/newly-registered-nurses/who-to-be-advised-by-early-career-nursing-group-15-10-2026/), 15 October).

At the Lampard inquiry in England, lawyers representing bereaved families called for Oxevision monitoring cameras to be stopped on mental-health inpatient wards, citing patient safety, privacy and the risk of remote monitoring replacing in-person care; the manufacturer disputed claims that the system was ineffective or unsafe ([read the report](https://www.theguardian.com/society/2026/oct/20/ban-intrusive-cameras-mental-health-wards-england-bereaved-families), 20 October).
