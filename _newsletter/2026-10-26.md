## Swiss health policy

The federal campaign against domestic, sexual and gender-based violence expanded its information and support services, adding multilingual and easy-to-read content, an online relationship check and free campaign materials ([read the release](https://www.admin.ch/en/newnsb/XXFGtx5Ikr82), 26 October).

Swissinfo reported that pharmaceutical companies continue to press for higher prices and broader reforms in Europe, including Switzerland, as US reference-price measures evolve; the analysis discusses possible implications for access to medicines and remaining uncertainty ([read the analysis](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/drug-pricing/pharma-pushes-for-higher-european-drug-prices-trump-or-no-trump/92242926), 21 October).

## Nursing and healthcare in Switzerland

A Swiss woman infected with Ebola in Nigeria was being brought to Switzerland for treatment. Nigerian authorities said she was stable; Swissinfo noted that Zurich and Geneva university hospitals are equipped as reference hospitals for Ebola ([read the report](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/foreign-affairs/swiss-ebola-patient-is-being-brought-back-to-switzerland/92274746), 26 October).

SRF Investigativ reported that Swissmedic did not request further reports from the manufacturer after serious problems with an experimental heart implant used at Zurich University Hospital, examining the regulator's and hospital's handling of the case ([read the investigation](https://www.srf.ch/news/schweiz/debakel-um-chirurg-maisano-usz-herzklinik-skandal-behoerde-wollte-nicht-zu-penetrant-sein), 26 October).

## Nursing and healthcare worldwide

Swissinfo reported that Ebola response in eastern Democratic Republic of the Congo is hampered by late access to treatment, misinformation, insecurity and gaps in contact tracing, drawing on an interview with a clinician from Swiss NGO Medair ([read the report](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/foreign-affairs/exploitation-and-misinformation-hinder-drc-ebola-response/92262382), 26 October).
